READ BEFORE GENERATING
  • PEOPLESome faces won't be accepted for generation — there's no way to check in advance, so a rejection doesn't mean you did anything wrong. Try a different photo of the same person if this happens.
  • MINORSPhotos of anyone under 18 cannot be used if you're generating from the EU, UK, or Switzerland.
  • LENGTHEvery memory comes out as one 10-second clip. Longer scenes aren't supported yet — think of it as a single vivid beat, not a full story.
  • CUTSIf your description implies multiple moments ("we studied, then ordered food, then fell asleep"), expect the model to pick one moment rather than cut between all three.
  • QUALITYOutput is 720p. It won't look like a phone-shot video — treat it as an impression of the memory, not a recreation of the original photo.
  • LANGUAGEDescriptions work best in English. Other languages may generate unpredictable results.
  • WATERMARKEvery clip carries an invisible AI watermark (SynthID). This can't be removed and isn't meant to be.
  • RETRYIf a generation fails or gets blocked, you won't be charged for it — but you will need to try again, possibly with a different photo or a softer description.
